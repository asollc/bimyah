# PRODUCT.md — Bimyah!

## Purpose
Bimyah! is a fast, no-turns card race game. Everyone plays simultaneously, so there is no waiting around for your turn. It delivers the same competitive fun as Uno for family, friends, party gamers, competitive card players, and Discord communities.

## Users
- Family and friends looking for quick competitive fun together
- Casual party gamers
- Competitive card players
- Discord communities playing together in voice/text

## Core loop
1. Each player gets personal piles (count varies by player count) plus a hand
2. Shared center cards sit face up
3. Play a card from hand onto a center card one rank higher or lower (aces wrap); the played card replaces the center
4. Swap cards between your piles and the center cards to build 4-of-a-kind sets (center works as intermediary for pile-to-pile swaps)
5. Tap SET to lock a completed 4-of-a-kind pile
6. First to complete all piles slaps BIMYAH! to win the round
7. Host sets a point target; round placements earn points; first to the target wins the match

## Differentiator
No turns. Every player acts at the same time, so the game never stalls waiting on someone. Speed and pattern recognition win, not turn order.
